### The part that reorders the plan

A single 2 ms arm — `sklearn_text|vectorizer=tfidf_word|clf=logreg|features=rules|feature_scale=maxabs`,
no base artifact, no recording, no server — reaches **349/415 = 84.1%** out of fold at stratified
seed 0, against the 3B's 325. The fusion is 351. And `feature_scale=maxabs` alone moves that
member by **35 rows**. So the fusion's +26 is not yet attributable to fusion; a competing
explanation worth 35 rows has not been excluded.

The fold rule does the same thing from the other side: the same member moves by **26 rows**
between stratified and hashed folding, which is the size of the whole claimed delta. Every
artifact now records `cv_rule` and `stratified`, and `--cv-rule` makes the rule an instrument.

Full write-up: `docs/fusion-2026-11-10.md`; artifacts in `bench/results/fusion-2026-11-10/` and
`bench/results/fusion-sensitivity-2026-11-10/`.

## Dropped by the plan, with the number that dropped them

- **Fine-tune an encoder.** A *frozen* 22.7M encoder already scores 78.74–81.64% on the 207 test
  rows against the 3B's 79.71%. There is no headroom.
- **Object-noun lexicons.** +13.0 points on v3, **−7.3 on transfer**: they memorise the generator.
- **The classical temperature edit.** Accuracy is invariant across T (it is a softmax-argmax
  identity), so the only lever is coverage, and chosen out-of-fold the cascade buys 0.0000 at
  p = 1.0. The corpus fix still ships, as observability.
- **Any weight-of-sweep tuning.** `w` is a knob; a peak-of-sweep number is the error that killed
  the earlier cascade.
