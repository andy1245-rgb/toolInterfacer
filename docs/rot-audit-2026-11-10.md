## 1. `classical` — the known case

**1.1 [B — CLOSED by `e9b3ca4`]** The corpus could not name a single candidate: fitted labels were
17 voice-assistant tools, options were the eight code ops, intersection empty, so `choice()` returned
`ChoiceAnswer(None, {uniform}, 0.125)` on all 415 rows. Evidence: `python3 /tmp/rot-audit/d1_coverage.py`
→ `fitted ∩ options = []`, `answered 0/415 abstained 415`. *Fix:* a corpus built from the v3 training
split; shipped with a generator that refuses a corpus whose labels miss the option set.

**1.2 [M — CLOSED as a symptom]** `temperature` was inert: 0.01 through 100 produced one distinct
confidence and zero answers, because the vocabulary was wrong before confidence mattered — and it is
the only setting anyone ever changed. Evidence at `HEAD`:
`python3 /tmp/rot-audit/temp_sweep.py` → `answered 0/415` at every temperature.
**After the revival it is no longer inert, and it is still unjustified:** it moves p_max (median
0.244 at T=1.0, 0.887 at T=0.2) and never moves the argmax, so it is a *coverage* knob with no
recorded run behind its shipped value of 1.0 — and at 1.0 the tier's p_max tops out at 0.499, below
any 0.70 gate. Evidence: `bench/harness` with `classical|temperature=…` over the eval split.

**1.3 [M — open, and the most consequential in the register]** **An abstention is manufactured into
a pick.** `run_pick` takes `top_k` candidates; with `top_k=1` an abstention becomes `[None]`, and
with `top_k=4` it becomes four named labels drawn from a uniform distribution. Evidence:
`python3 /tmp/rot-audit/abstain_consumer.py` → `run_pick(top_k=1) returns [None]`,
`run_pick(top_k=4) returns ['filesys_read', 'filesys_glob', 'filesys_grep', 'decide_pick']`.
*Smallest fix:* `run_pick` should refuse a candidate list whose first element is `None`, and
`ChoiceAnswer` with `choice=None` should never reach it.

**1.4 The 93.19% claim — verified, not a finding.** `bench/classical_baseline.py` reproduces
93.19% / 0.60 / 0.00 to the digit into a scratch directory, and the record exists at
`bench/results/repro2-2026-11-03/classical_382.json`.

## 2. `heuristic_micro` — advertised behaviour that cannot fire

**2.1 [M]** One of its four branches emits `hits["filesys"]`, which is not one of the eight option
names, and the filter `if label in criteria` drops it, so the branch answers `None`. Evidence:
`make_backend('heuristic_micro').choice('read /etc/hosts','',{'filesys_read':'r','filesys_glob':'g'})`
→ `None`, while adding a fake `filesys` key to the criteria makes it answer `filesys` at 0.99.
*Fix:* emit the three concrete `filesys_*` ops with a split rule, or delete the branch.

**2.2 [M]** `_PATH` requires a leading `/`, so it never matches the corpus: **0 of 415** rows.
Evidence: `python3 /tmp/rot-audit/d4_reachability.py` (path section) →
`rows whose state matches _PATH: 0 of 415`.

**2.3 [M]** Its reported confidence is not the peak of the distribution it returns: a 50/50 answer
is reported at 0.99, and its vector sums to 1.000006. Evidence:
`python3 /tmp/rot-audit/heuristic_conf.py`.

## 3. `fixture` — the shipped `noul` backend

**3.1 [M]** `noul` returns **0.9 for every question ever asked**, and the relevance gate it feeds
(`gate_noul: 0.75`) therefore never closes: a sourdough recipe, a 404 page and a listicle are all
KEPT. Evidence: `python3 /tmp/rot-audit/noul_probe.py`. This is the known case's sibling — a gate
whose answer is constant is not a gate.

**3.2 [M]** `choice` fabricates `ids[0]` at 0.95 confidence while its own probabilities are uniform
at 0.125. Evidence: `/tmp/rot-audit/raw §3.2`.

**3.3 [U]** `fixture_path: ""` resolves to the repo root and silently means "no map": the map is
empty and nothing warns.

## 8. The tests

**8.1 [M — CLOSED by `e9b3ca4`]** 146 tests were green at `HEAD` while the tier was dead. **8.2 [M]**
`tests/test_classical_backend.py:142-145` asserted the corpus held **18 labels and `__none__`** —

tests exist; they are acceptable and labelled.

## 13. What I would spend on, in order

1. **§1.3, an abstention becoming a pick.** It is the one finding that can turn "no opinion" into a
   wrong action, and it is a two-line guard in `run_pick`.
2. **§3.1, the constant `noul`.** A gate that cannot close is the known case wearing a different
   hat, and it is the only relevance filter on the default extraction route.
3. **§2.1/§2.2, `heuristic_micro`'s dead third.** The pre-gate is the cheapest tier and one of its
   four branches cannot fire while another never matches the traffic.
4. **§6.1, the escape hatch.** A fresh install is told to use a backend that (until `e9b3ca4`)
   answered nothing.
5. Everything else is a register entry, not a task.
